Our Story

A Hidden Paradise,
Far from the Noise

The Resthouse Laiya is not just a place to stay — it's a place to breathe. Nestled on a private 9-hectare beachfront estate in Laiya, San Juan, Batangas, we offer what few resorts can: genuine solitude, uncrowded white sand, and the kind of stillness that resets the soul.

No strangers. No crowds. Just your family, your friends, and the sound of the sea. Watch sea turtles nest at dusk. Wake up to birds you've never heard before. This is Laiya, done differently.

The Experience

Live Slowly.
Feel Everything.

Activities

Discover What Laiya
Has to Offer

From the calm of your private beach to the thrill of nearby adventures — Laiya has a pace for every kind of guest.

At The Resthouse
🏖️

Private Beach & Swimming

Your own stretch of Laiya's white sand — no strangers, no vendors. Kayak, float, or simply do nothing at all.

🦜

Bird Watching & Turtle Nesting

Spot kingfishers, herons, and bee-eaters. At dusk, witness sea turtles nesting — one of Laiya's rarest gifts.

🌅

Sunrise Paddles & Sunset Bonfires

Kayak at golden hour. End the day with a beach bonfire — Laiya sunsets are a masterpiece every evening.

Nearby Laiya
🪂

Laiya Adventure Park

Zipline, ATV rides, wall climbing, giant swing, and free fall set inside a jungle-clad hill.

~10 min drive
⛵

Catamaran & Mangrove Tours

Charter a catamaran for a full-day bay tour with snorkeling. Or take a motorized outrigger through mangrove rivers.

Arrangeable
☕

Laiya Food & Culture Trail

Try lomi, bulalo, Batangas kapeng barako, and coconut lambanog — a short drive opens a full local food trail.

~15 min drive
Wake up to waves, take your coffee with the birds, sleep with the sound of nature — and do it all over again.
